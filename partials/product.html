About ICWS

A clearer way through college buildings.

Finding a room can be difficult when you are new to a campus, moving between floors, or looking for a specific service. ICWS is a proposed indoor wayfinding application for students, staff, and visitors.

01

Find the right place

Search for classrooms, offices, facilities, and college services.

02

Know where you are

Use indoor positioning and confirm or correct your starting location.

03

Move with confidence

Follow directions that account for floors, route choices, and accessibility needs.

Meet ABC-chan

Your intelligent indoor navigation assistant.

ABC-chan is the assistant that makes routes in ICWS. It helps users find destinations, supports route selection, and guides them through the college, including floor transitions.

The website presents ABC-chan as part of the proposed system. Advanced AI capabilities should not be understood as already implemented unless confirmed by the project.

Application features

Every part of the indoor journey.

ICWS brings destination search, indoor location, and route guidance together. Positioning, maps, and integrations depend on future campus data and deployment.

01

Room search

Search for classrooms, offices, and teaching spaces by their name, number, or category.

02

Facilities and services

Find facilities—physical places such as a library, toilet, or cafeteria—and services such as student support, reception, or the IT helpdesk.

03

Building and floor identification

Identify the destination's building and floor automatically from the available campus information.

04

Wi-Fi indoor positioning

Use Wi-Fi access point information to estimate a user's indoor location, subject to campus coverage and positioning accuracy.

05

Manual location correction

Let users review a detected starting point and adjust it when the estimate does not match their location.

06

Indoor floor maps

Show rooms, route context, and floor transitions on indoor maps supplied for supported buildings.

07

Turn-by-turn navigation

Follow route guidance through the building and receive information when arriving at the destination.

08

Lift and staircase selection

For another-floor destinations, choose a lift or stairs where the building route data supports both options.

09

Floor changing

Navigate to the selected lift or stairs, change floors, confirm the new floor, then continue to the destination.

10

Barrier-free routing

Support wheelchair-friendly route choices that prioritise lifts and ramps and avoid stairs when route data allows.

11

Authorised NFC door access

Describe an NFC-compatible hand-off to electronic doors for authorised users, subject to campus access systems and permissions.

12

Room information

Review useful destination details before setting off.

13

User profile and settings

Set route preferences, including accessibility needs, for a more suitable way through the building.

Accessibility

A route should fit the traveller.

The proposed barrier-free routing can prioritise accessible paths, lifts, and ramps while avoiding stairs where route information permits.

  • Wheelchair-friendly navigation

    Route preferences can guide selection of paths and floor transitions suitable for wheelchair users.

  • Lifts and ramps

    Use mapped lifts and ramps as alternatives when the building route data includes them.

  • Avoiding stairs

    Offer a route that avoids stairs where a connected accessible route is available.

  • Accessible facilities

    Provide information that helps users locate accessible facilities in supported buildings.

Project information

A focused proposal for clearer campus movement.

Background and problem

College buildings can be difficult to navigate, especially for people unfamiliar with their rooms, facilities, services, or floor layouts. Outdoor navigation alone does not describe the route once inside.

Objectives

Help users find destinations, understand their indoor location, follow turn-by-turn directions, and make informed choices for floor changes and accessible routes.

Scope

The proposed system covers destination search, indoor positioning, map-based routing, room information, accessibility choices, and authorised NFC door integrations.

Stakeholders

Intended users include students, teachers, staff, visitors, and college management. Facilities teams may be involved in map and access-system integrations.

Limitations

Coverage and accuracy depend on building maps, Wi-Fi infrastructure, route data, device support, and campus integrations. NFC access requires compatible systems and authorisation.

Proposed benefits

ICWS aims to reduce uncertainty when moving indoors and make it easier to find rooms, college resources, and suitable routes.
