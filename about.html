About ICWS

Beyond the
building entrance.

A street map can take you to college. Finding a classroom inside can mean an unfamiliar corridor, a different floor and a choice of stairs or lift.

The project.

Objective
Bring room search, indoor maps and step-by-step directions into one application, with ABC-chan helping travellers choose a route.
Community
Students, lecturers, staff and visitors need to reach unfamiliar rooms. Campus facilities teams and college management would maintain maps and access policies.
Scope
The prototype explores indoor positioning, floor changes, accessible routes and authorised door access. Reliable campus data and compatible hardware would be needed for deployment.
Expected benefit
Clearer room and floor information could reduce wrong turns and help people plan a route that suits their mobility needs.
