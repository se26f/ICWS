# ICWS studio studies

The five JPG studies were rendered at 1200 × 620 before the primary pages were edited. They use the supplied device PNGs, ABC artwork and local Inter. The daylight architectural set was generated separately and contains no application UI. The HTML source keeps typography, devices and art separable.

## Observations from the reference review

| Rendered reference | Observation | ICWS application |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [iPhone](https://www.apple.com/iphone/) | The small product lineup gives way to a much larger single product scene. The calls to action occupy little space. | Give the route phone priority and use a smaller search phone to explain how the journey starts. |
| [MacBook Pro](https://www.apple.com/macbook-pro/) | A restrained dark field and aligned product comparisons communicate relationships even at different image sizes. | Reserve a consistent dark field for the system map. Align related components and make requests and responses explicit. |
| [Apple Watch](https://www.apple.com/apple-watch/) | The small overview and close product studies serve different reading distances. | Limit normal device images to their native detail. Provide a separate enlarged viewer and direct screen selection. |
| [Linear](https://linear.app/) | Compact navigation and quiet typography let the working interface carry the explanation below the introduction. | Keep the shared header shallow. Let the four chapters and the technical connections supply the page structure. |

Each external reference was opened in Chromium at 1280 × 800 and captured at two scroll positions. Third-party imagery is excluded from the shipped website.

## Baseline and intended changes

| Page | Baseline observation | Target composition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Home | A dense gridded backdrop sits behind three similar phones. | Daylight physical campus model, one dominant phone, short headline, route strip. |
| Features | One switcher hides most of the features and gives each the same composition. | A search exhibition followed by positioning, routing and access studies. |
| App Showcase | Chapter lists compete with a framed phone panel. | Product held in view across four chapters, short synchronized screen copy and direct access. |
| Technology | Equally weighted points flatten components and data into a single chain. | Distinct positioning, route and credential flows with direction and payloads. |
| About Us | A dark architectural rendering gives the project story the same weight as Home. | A lighter editorial introduction, compact project facts, honest team placeholders and local feedback. |

## Scale and movement

Keep a shared 1180px maximum text grid with responsive gutters. Set explanatory type around 16–20px and controls around 13–16px. Apply larger display type to the main launch line; let chapter and feature headings drop a level. In the 1200px hero study the navigation device is 405px tall, the search device 252px. Pale floor plans carry a cobalt route and a red destination. Animate the route in floor coordinates so the marker remains on the corridor during the level change.
