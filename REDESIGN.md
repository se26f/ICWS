# ICWS product presentation

The entrance-to-W201 journey connects five static pages. Match the saved compositions in `design/concepts/`. Use the original screens for every product view and the supplied ABC artwork wherever the assistant appears.

## Art direction

Home opens in daylight. Place a complete navigation phone at approximately 400px high and a smaller search phone in a generated architectural model. Stage the action on the right and keep the text area quiet. On a narrow screen, stack the introduction above the product and give the route more room than the background. The architectural photograph illustrates an imagined college interior.

Use local Inter, porcelain white, light blue-grey, navy ink, and a restrained cobalt route. Reserve dark navy for technical data flow. Align navigation, captions and content to a shared container up to 1180px. Keep control labels short, retain 44px touch targets for the main controls and use a visible focus ring. Preserve native device proportions.

## Page compositions

- **Home** pairs the product photograph with a tactile two-floor map. A blue marker traces the entrance, lift and arrival. Provide replay, scroll progression and a complete reduced-motion illustration.
- **App Showcase** holds the phone in view through Discover, Locate, Navigate and Arrive. Direct controls and an accessible viewer give access to every supplied screen.
- **Features** exhibits real search, positioning and routing screens alongside explanations of access preferences and campus integration.
- **Technology** uses labelled, directed connections to distinguish campus data, route calculation, the return to the mobile app and electronic door authorisation.
- **About Us** combines a short project introduction, objectives, stakeholders, three unfilled team roles and local form validation.

## Motion and evidence

Use short, finite product entrances and user-driven progression. Keep text and primary actions available immediately. Retain a readable route when motion or scripting is disabled. Compare viewport and full-page captures at all seven documented sizes against the concept studies. Capture the intermediate floors, chapter changes, enlarged phone and mobile menu.
